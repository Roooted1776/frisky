Roadside Aid

Call 911 first. Open only what you need. NFC setup is not on this card.

Crash & Head
  1. Call 911 — give exact location and number of people
  2. Hazards on. Do not move them unless fire or traffic danger
  3. Keep head/neck still. Control bleeding with firm pressure
Bleeding
  1. Press hard with both hands — do not lift to check
  2. Add cloth on top if soaked. Limb: tourniquet 2–3 in above
  3. Note the time. Stay on the line with 911
CPR / Choking
  1. No breathing/pulse: hard compressions 100–120/min
  2. Choking, cannot cough: 5 back blows, then 5 abdominal thrusts
  3. Alternate until clear or they become unresponsive — then CPR
Seizure
  1. Time it. Clear sharp objects. Do not restrain
  2. Nothing in the mouth. Cushion the head
  3. Call 911 if first seizure, over 5 minutes, injury, or in water
