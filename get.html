Set up your band
  1. 1Open RedMed on iPhone and fill in your allergies, meds, and contacts.
  2. 2Hold your band to the top of your iPhone once — the chip stores your emergency card.
  3. 3Done. Anyone taps the band — their phone opens Call 911 and your critical info. No app needed.

Passive 13.56 MHz HF NFC only — no battery, no Bluetooth pair. 16″ standoff: won't fire at hand distance; phone powers the band only on Write/Scan or a deliberate ~cm tap. RedMed is not a medical device. In an emergency, call 911.
