# Clinical Safety Officer — appointment

**Standard:** DCB0129 (Clinical Risk Management: its Application in the
Manufacture of Health IT Systems)

## Role

The CSO owns clinical risk for RedMed health IT manufacture: hazard
identification, risk evaluation, residual risk acceptance, and the clinical
safety case. They can veto releases that change clinical risk without controls.

## Requirements (practical)

- Competent in clinical risk management for health IT (clinician or equivalent
  experience accepted under current DTAC posture — NHS Digital-specific training
  is no longer a hard named requirement in the refreshed DTAC, but competence
  still matters).
- Independence to escalate to leadership.
- Named in the DTAC clinical safety section and in the safety case report.
