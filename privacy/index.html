RedMed Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026. This is the public policy for App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1. The same terms appear in the app under Help → Privacy.

What we collect

RedMed does not collect your medical ID, location, Health data, Face ID biometrics, or usage analytics. There is no RedMed profile server and no account.

You may type a medical ID on your iPhone. That stays in this phone’s Keychain. If you write an NFC band, a copy lives on that chip so a passerby tap can open the card. If you allow Location on the 911 tab, coordinates stay on screen for you to read to dispatch. Optional Apple Health import (when enabled) reads only birth date and blood type on this phone. Motion for the crash alarm is processed in RAM and not stored.

If you email help.RedMed@gmail.com we see the address and message you send.

How we use it

On-device data is used only to show your ID, optionally write a band, show 911 coordinates, or sound a local alarm. We do not use data for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Third parties

No ad networks, analytics SDKs, or data brokers. We do not sell personal information. Apple may process Face ID, Location, Health, or NFC on the device under Apple’s rules. Anyone who taps your band can read the card — that is the product, not a RedMed upload.

Retention and deletion

We keep no profile. On the phone: Help → Erase all RedMed data (Face ID), or delete the app. On the band: overwrite or discard it. We cannot wipe a band remotely. Support email is kept only long enough to reply, then deleted.

Withdraw consent

iOS Settings → RedMed to revoke Location, Health, or Face ID. 911 still works without Location. You can leave profile fields blank. Erase removes on-device RedMed data.
