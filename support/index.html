RedMed Support

RedMed is a personal medical ID on your iPhone. There is no account and no RedMed server for your profile.

In an emergency

Call your local emergency number first. RedMed does not dispatch help and is not a medical device. Coordinates on Find Help stay on this phone for you to read — they are not sent to 911.

App help

  • Edit, Save, and Erase use Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. App launch, 911, Aid, and NFC write do not.
  • A passerby who taps a written band opens the card in the browser with no login and no Face ID.
  • Help inside the app: Privacy, Terms, and Security.
