How It Works

A quick walkthrough of the RedMed app for new users.

In short Fill out your emergency profile once in the app. On this phone you can preview what a rescuer sees, call 911 with live GPS, and follow roadside aid steps — all local, no RedMed account or server.

1. Set up your profile

Open the RedMed tab and tap Edit. Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode when asked. Add your name, birth date, and blood type, then list any allergies, medications, and conditions a first responder should know. Add emergency contacts with phone numbers. Tap Save when done.

2. Preview what others see

On the RedMed tab, tap Preview scanner to open the same read-only RedMed / 911 / Aid shell a stranger would use. Confirm your details look right. There is no Edit control in that preview — editing stays on your unlocked owner app.

3. In an emergency

Use the 911 tab for one-tap dialing and live GPS coordinates to read off to a dispatcher. Use the Aid tab for step-by-step guidance on bleeding, CPR, shock, and other roadside situations while help is on the way.

4. Bracelet writing (coming later)

Writing your profile to a passive NFC bracelet needs Apple’s NFC Tag Reading capability, which requires a paid Apple Developer Program membership. That owner-only write flow is parked for now.

When it returns, a stranger who taps the band opens a read-only emergency card in their browser with the same three tabs as this app — RedMed, 911, and Aid — no app install and no Edit. Until then, use the in-app profile and Preview scanner on this phone.

5. Keeping it accurate

If your medical details change, open Edit again, save, and re-check Preview scanner. See our Privacy Policy for where your data lives.
