How It Works

Effective Date August 2026
Document Version 3.3 (United States)

Setup for the local-only RedMed EMS-assist app and passive NFC ID bracelet (United States)

Opening RedMed Local-only EMS assist: quicker Aid, help reaching 911, and a fast local handoff of patient ID / PII to EMS or the hospital (band tap / card — not a RedMed cloud). HIPAA-aligned for RedMed as operator; not a medical device; no promised outcome.

iPhone owners: Open the RedMed app.

Any phone (EMT / helper / Android / desktop): Open the emergency card — RedMed · 911 · Aid in the browser. No app install.

In an emergency in the United States, call 911 first — before reading Aid content or writing a band.
