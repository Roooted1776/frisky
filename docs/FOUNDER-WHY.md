# Founder why (careful copy)

**Audience:** About page, station leave-behind insert, Arrival Day Pack
card / one-pager. Paste only. Not a chart. Not ads.

**Rule:** Max’s history is the **why** — **categories only**, no chart dump
(no med list, no doses, no lab table, no visit notes, no sensitive MH detail
beyond high-level category if you must mention it at all). Public copy stays
inside the FDA / wellness wall
([`FDA-CLAIMS-WALL.md`](FDA-CLAIMS-WALL.md)).

**Product locks in every surface:** free · no ads · no paywall · no
subscription · local-only (Keychain / band `#d=` — no RedMed profile server).

---

## Why (categories only)

RedMed exists because the founder already lives the failure mode the band is
for: found down, phone locked or useless, a stranger needs the **categories
that matter in ten seconds** — not a hospital chart.

Categories that drove the build (name them; do **not** expand into a dump):

| Category | Why it belongs on an ICE band |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Epilepsy / seizures | Rescue timing; strangers need the word on the wrist |
| Neurological hardware (e.g. VP shunt / Chiari history) | Head injury / ER path changes |
| Bleeding risk / low platelets (ITP) | No NSAIDs; bleed risk is not obvious from the outside |
| Complex meds + allergy confusion | False or noisy chart lines confuse ER; the band carries what the wearer typed |

Do not paste MyChart exports, Daily Meds sheets, or full condition lists into
About / station / Pack copy. Categories → product. Chart stays private.

### About (short)

```
RedMed started because the founder needed a medical ID that works when the
phone does not — epilepsy, neurological hardware, bleeding risk, and a med
picture that is easy to get wrong in a chart dump.

So RedMed is local-only. Your profile stays on your iPhone and, when written,
on a passive NFC band. Free. No ads. No paywall. No subscription. No RedMed
server for your card.

Tap the band. Any phone. Safari opens what you entered. Not a hospital chart.
Not a medical device. Call emergency services first.
```
