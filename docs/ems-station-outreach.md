## Gate

Until the company gate is green (LLC, IP assignment, liability insurance, FDA counsel on facility-issued bands, BAA decision, invoice path):

- **Do:** teach staff to tap a band a patient already wears
- **Do not:** outbound to materials management / GPO / hospital admin, or paid media aimed at facilities

## 60-second brief

If you see a black wristband with the RedMed logo-print face: hold the **top** of the phone to that plate, about an inch or two. Safari opens a medical card. No app. No login. Allergies, meds, conditions, contacts, Call.

Walk-by will not fire. Payment terminals will not open this card. Self-reported ICE, not a hospital record. Call emergency services first.
