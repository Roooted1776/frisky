16. Bracelets and Other Physical Items

If you buy a bracelet from us or an authorised seller:

  • Your medical information is written to a passive HF NFC chip at 13.56 MHz (ISO 14443 / NTAG). The chip has no battery, no Bluetooth radio, and no sensors — your phone powers it briefly on tap or write only. It does not track you, monitor you, or keep a paired RF link. Casual walk-by distance will not fire the band; only a deliberate close antenna tap (~1–2 inches) reads it. That is HF NFC physics, not a tunable range setting.
  • The chip can be read by any smartphone that deliberately taps it. That is deliberate, so that responders need no app and no password. Payment terminals speak a different protocol (EMV) and do not open the RedMed card — that is not a distance control.
  • Information on the chip is not encrypted. Treat the bracelet as you would a conventional medical ID card.
  • You are responsible for keeping the chip contents current by rewriting the band after you change your profile.
